# Dumping Station

"How do you actually hire 5,000 people?"

Open the hiring portal on screen. Show the 46,000 candidate number.
Explain qualification, filtering and your recruitment engine.

"Hiring people is easy. Making them dangerous is hard."

Show the training platform. Explain how AI assigns training, tests
people and connects them with the team.

"Here's how we manage 126 salespeople."

Show attendance, leaderboard, payroll, targets and QA. Numbers make this
one feel real.

"I can't listen to thousands of sales calls. So we built this."

Show your call-scoring system. Explain how every call gets evaluated and
how that feeds coaching.

"What happens after sales start coming in?"

Show the fulfillment dashboard and how AI helps your fulfillment team
manage growing client volume.

"Nobody talks about this part of scaling."

Open the expense portal. Show how you track money coming in, payroll and
departmental expenses. Talk about why financial visibility becomes
survival at scale.

"Why I don't want AI to replace my employees."

Your angle: I want AI to make each employee more capable. That's a much
more interesting founder perspective.

"The biggest mistake I made while growing from 10 to 200+ people."

Pick one actual mistake. This should be personal and specific, not
motivational.

"What nobody tells you about a PKR 10 million payroll."

Responsibility, cash flow, performance expectations and knowing hundreds
of families depend on the machine working.

"Why I'm building my own software instead of buying everything."

Show one internal tool and explain the exact operational problem that
forced you to build it.
