# PractiScale Company Brain

## What PractiScale does

PractiScale helps healthcare businesses grow through professional referral relationships.

The core idea is simple:

Many healthcare operators do not need another generic list of leads. They need the right people in their local healthcare ecosystem to know them, trust them, and think of them when a relevant patient or opportunity appears.

PractiScale identifies, researches, qualifies, and helps create introductions to relevant referral partners. The healthcare business then builds and owns the relationship.

## What PractiScale is not

Do not casually position PractiScale as:
- a lead-list company
- a mass-email agency
- a generic advertising agency
- a guaranteed-patient service
- a company that controls whether a third party sends referrals

The distinction matters.

A list gives someone contact information.

A professional introduction gives the client a starting point for a real relationship.

## The underlying belief

Healthcare growth is heavily influenced by trust.

Ads can create attention.

Relationships can create recognition, confidence, introductions, and repeat opportunities.

A useful shorthand:

"Being known gets attention. Being trusted gets the call."

Another:

"Don't rent everything. Build something you own too."
