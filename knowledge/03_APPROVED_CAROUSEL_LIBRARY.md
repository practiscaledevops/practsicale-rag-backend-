# 15 GOLD STANDARD QUOTES

These are style anchors. Do not copy them mechanically.

## Approved-style anchors

"You don't outgrow people. You outgrow what you tolerated."

Why it works:
A familiar phrase is reversed into a sharper observation about standards.

"Stop explaining your standards. Start enforcing them."

Why it works:
Two commands. No filler. Behavior replaces explanation.

"Hiring gives you people. Systems turn them into a company."

Why it works:
Clear distinction between headcount and organization.

"Growth without systems doesn't create a bigger company. It creates bigger chaos."

Why it works:
The expected outcome is reversed. Growth becomes a stress test.

"The office is the result. Sales was the engine."

Why it works:
Visible success is separated from the mechanism that produced it.

"Solve once. Document once. Systemize forever."

Why it works:
Three-stage rhythm. It converts an operating belief into a memorable rule.

## What these examples have in common

They do not:
- open with context
- explain themselves for five sentences
- use marketing adjectives
- sound motivational for the sake of motivation

They do:
- contain a belief
- create contrast
- compress the lesson
- use ordinary words
- end strongly

## Generation rule

When asked for 10 quotes, produce 10 genuinely different ideas.

Do not take one idea and swap synonyms.

A good set should feel like ten lessons extracted from the source, not ten captions generated from one lesson.
