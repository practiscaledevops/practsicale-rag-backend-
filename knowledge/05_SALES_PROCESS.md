# Offers and Pricing

This file contains the current internal copywriting reference. Because offers evolve, never override a newer fact supplied in the current prompt.

## 1. Patient Referral System Buildout

### Positioning

A lower-ticket research and strategy product that helps a healthcare operator understand the referral ecosystem available in their territory.

It is often used as the first purchase before a larger done-for-you engagement.

### Current tiers

Standard: $300
- target delivery: 5 business days

Priority: $500
- target delivery: 48 hours

### Core promise

Build a territory-specific referral-partner map and identify viable categories the business can pursue.

Current approved guarantee:
- 3 or more named viable referral categories, or a full refund

Do not turn this into a guarantee of patients, rides, revenue, signed partners, or referrals.

### Sales posture

This is not supposed to be sold through desperation.

If $300 is financially uncomfortable for the prospect, the sales posture is to warn them away rather than force the transaction.

## 2. Verified Referral Partner Introductions

### Positioning

PractiScale identifies and introduces relevant professional referral partners.

This is not a purchased patient lead.

The value is access to a relevant professional relationship that the client can continue developing.

### Current pricing reference

Setup: $500
- includes the first 5 verified introductions

Additional approved partner:
- $100 each

Partners are generally purchased in prepaid batches.

### Approval logic

A client has a defined approval/rejection window. Current reference:
- 48 hours

A rejected partner within the applicable policy may be replaced.

Do not summarize contract mechanics more aggressively than the actual agreement.

## 3. Medical Billing / RCM

Current pricing reference:
- under $5,000 in collections: $500 flat
- $5,000 to $15,000: $750 flat
- above $15,000: 5%

Use only when the copy is specifically about billing.

## 4. Credentialing

Current reference ranges:
- commercial payer: $150 to $300 per provider, per payer
- government payer: $250 to $500 per provider, per payer

Treat these as reference ranges, not universal quotes.

## Offer ladder

A common sales path is:

Cold outreach or content
→ conversation
→ Patient Referral System Buildout / research
→ deeper strategy conversation
→ higher-ticket implementation or referral-partner engagement

Do not collapse every stage into one pitch.

A prospect who is merely curious should not receive the same message as someone who has already purchased research.

## NEMT-specific positioning

The emotional destination is not "more leads."

A stronger outcome is:
- recurring facility relationships
- being known by facilities
- more control over where trips come from
- less dependence on random broker legs

Approved conceptual language:

"Standing, recurring trips assigned directly by facilities that know you by name, a fuller schedule you control instead of constantly chasing broker legs at broker rates."

Avoid guaranteeing that this outcome will occur.
