# Afra Writing DNA

This file captures writing preferences only. It does not contain private personal information.

## What Afra tends to reward

### Strong thinking before strong wording

If the idea is weak, do not polish it.

Fix:
- positioning
- angle
- argument
- proof
- contrast
- CTA

Then write.

### Directness

Afra usually prefers the version that reaches the interesting point faster.

### Specificity

Numbers, situations, names, mechanisms, and concrete details beat vague claims.

Example:

Generic:
"Our team members can earn great incomes."

Specific:
"Hassan's paycheck reached PKR 11 lakh."

The specific version creates a question in the reader's mind.

### Human imperfection

Especially for Pakistani social content, writing can feel natural and spoken rather than perfectly polished.

Do not deliberately add spelling mistakes. The goal is human rhythm, not sloppiness.

### Founder perspective

CEO/personal-brand content should often sound like someone who has actually lived the problem.

Prefer:
"What I learned after..."
"I made this mistake..."
"We tried..."
"Then I realized..."

Avoid pretending the founder personally experienced something that has not been supplied.

### Lessons over showing off

A number is stronger when it leads somewhere.

Weak:
"We have a huge team."

Better:
Explain what building that team taught the founder about hiring, leadership, systems, or sales.

### Emotional but controlled

Afra likes copy that can hit emotionally, but dislikes fake inspiration.

### Pakistani relatability

For Pakistan-facing content:
- simple English
- Roman Urdu or Urdu-English mix when requested
- culturally recognizable examples
- no imported Silicon Valley tone

## Default content structure

A strong founder short-form structure:

Hook
→ real situation
→ tension
→ insight
→ lesson
→ memorable close
→ CTA only if needed

## RAW framework for story-led content

### Report
What actually happened?

### Analysis
What did it teach?

### Why
Why does that lesson matter?

Push beyond the surface:
fact
→ experience
→ emotion
→ fear
→ identity
→ belief

Publish the deepest truthful layer that is appropriate for the audience.

## LIFE story buckets

Useful story organization:
- Love
- Identity
- Fitness
- Earnings

These can then be tagged with themes such as:
- leadership
- hiring
- sales
- marketing
- productivity
- money
- mindset
- failure
- success

For company copywriters, use only stories and details explicitly approved for public use.
