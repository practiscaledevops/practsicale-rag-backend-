## Core delivery areas

PractiScale delivery can involve:

- Client onboarding
- Referral-partner research
- Prospecting/outreach
- Verification
- Referral-partner introductions
- Client success
- Quality assurance
- CRM operations
- Replacement/tracking
- Billing support
- Credentialing

## Referral partner fulfillment

The objective is not simply to produce lists.

For done-for-you referral-partner services, value comes from researching, contacting, qualifying, verifying, and connecting relevant referral sources based on the client's specialty and territory.

## Client communication

Client success has included onboarding and recurring client calls.

Operational policies, replacement terms, approval windows, and billing terms should always come from the latest approved agreement or operations document.

## AI Brain relevance

Operations knowledge matters because sales and marketing outputs must match what the company can actually fulfill.

The AI should never make a promise merely because it sounds persuasive.

Before generating a claim about delivery time, guarantee, replacement, quantity, or result, retrieve the latest approved offer or policy source.
