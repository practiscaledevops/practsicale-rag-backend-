# Prompts

I will share a raw video or video transcript, which may be in Urdu,
English, or a mix of both.

Analyze the actual message, story, examples, and lessons from the video
first. Then repurpose it into English content for Instagram.

For every video, create the following:

## 1. Instagram Caption

Write one Instagram SEO-friendly caption based on the video.

Start with a strong hook.

Preserve my original opinion, personality, and core message.

Don't simply translate or summarize the transcript.

Make it sound natural, sharp, and founder-led.

Use relevant searchable keywords naturally.

End with a suitable CTA only when the video calls for one.

Add relevant hashtags.

## 2. 10 Carousel Ideas

Create 10 different carousel concepts from the video.

Do NOT make every carousel the same length. Randomly vary them between
approximately 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 slides.

For each carousel:

Give me the title/hook.

Write the actual copy for every slide.

Keep each slide concise enough to work visually on Instagram.

Explore different angles from the same video rather than repeating the
same lesson 10 times.

Use stories, contrarian ideas, frameworks, mistakes, comparisons,
questions, lists, and lessons where appropriate.

## 3. 10 Standalone Quotes

Extract or develop 10 strong English quotes based on the thinking in the
video.

The quotes should:

Work independently without needing the video for context.

Be short enough for quote graphics.

Sound sharp, memorable, and original.

Preserve the philosophy behind what I actually said.

Avoid generic motivational quotes.

Also identify the 3 strongest quotes for standalone Instagram posts.

## 4. 5 Follow-Up Video Ideas

Give me 5 new videos I can record based on ideas, stories, questions,
objections, or lessons inside the original video.

For each one, provide:

Video topic

Opening hook

Main angle

3 to 5 talking points

Suggested closing line

Don't just suggest different versions of the original video. Expand the
idea into new conversations.

## Content Style

My content is founder-led and based on real experiences building teams,
sales, systems, leadership, hiring, management, and business growth.

Keep the rawness and conviction of my original thinking, but make the
English clearer and more articulate.

Do not turn everything into generic motivational content.

When I use a real number, employee story, company experience, analogy,
mistake, or unusual opinion, prioritize it. Those details make the
content believable.

If something I say is exaggerated, factually questionable, unnecessarily
offensive, or could damage the message, preserve the underlying point
but improve how it is communicated.

The goal is not to make me sound polished.

The goal is to make my raw thinking impossible to ignore.
