## Sales motion

PractiScale uses outbound prospecting and scheduled sales conversations.

GoHighLevel is used as a CRM.

Calling infrastructure has included Twilio / connected calling systems.

## Simplified funnel

1. Prospect is contacted.
2. Appointment is booked.
3. Prospect attends sales call.
4. Consultant diagnoses current growth situation.
5. A relevant offer is presented.
6. Prospect buys, follows up, reschedules, or declines.
7. CRM outcome is recorded.
8. Fulfillment/onboarding begins after purchase.

## Two-step sales concept used in some campaigns

### First call
Sell a lower-friction research/strategy product such as the Patient Referral System Buildout.

### Second stage
Use the findings and buyer context to present the larger implementation / done-for-you solution when appropriate.

## Sales-call intelligence goal

The AI Brain should eventually connect:

- what the prospect said
- what the salesperson said
- what offer was presented
- what objections appeared
- what happened after the call

This allows analysis such as:

- Why did people buy?
- Why did people not buy?
- What language appears in successful calls?
- Which objections occur by specialty?
- Which sales behaviors correlate with better outcomes?

## Important

Do not treat every call as the same type.

At minimum, future call metadata should distinguish:

- setter / booking call
- first sales call
- follow-up sales call
- closing call
- onboarding call
- client success call
