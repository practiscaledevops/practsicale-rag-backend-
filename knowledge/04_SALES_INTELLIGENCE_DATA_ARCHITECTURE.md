## Videos I would make from this learning

## Video 1: "I cried in front of a client."

Hook: "I run a company with hundreds of people, and I once cried in
front of a client."

Angle: Remove the fake invincibility around entrepreneurship.

Talking points:

Multiple problems were hitting simultaneously.

Accounts had been closed.

Money and disputes were creating pressure.

You reached your emotional limit.

One bad moment didn't mean the mission was wrong.

Closing: "Being a CEO doesn't remove your humanity. It just gives your
humanity bigger problems to carry."

## Video 2: "Don't make decisions on your worst day."

Hook: "Your worst day is probably the worst day to redesign your entire
life."

Angle: Separate emotional state from strategic decisions.

Talking points:

Something goes badly.

Emotion makes the future look like the present.

Suddenly the whole dream looks stupid.

Stabilize yourself first.

Then evaluate the business rationally.

Closing: "Fix today's problem before you cancel tomorrow's dream."

## Video 3: "Maybe your dream isn't too big."

Hook: "When you're struggling, people will tell you to dream smaller."

Angle: There is a difference between an unrealistic strategy and
temporary exhaustion.

Talking points:

Big goals should absolutely be challenged.

Numbers and reality matter.

But stress alone isn't evidence the vision is wrong.

Change the strategy when necessary.

Don't automatically change the destination.

Closing: "Sometimes you need a better plan, not a smaller ambition."

## Video 4: "What if Amazon had Instagram from Day 1?"

Hook: "Imagine watching Amazon being built every single day instead of
reading the success story afterward."

Angle: We judge businesses retrospectively and remove all uncertainty
from their stories.

Talking points:

Today we know which companies survived.

Their founders didn't know the ending while building them.

A terrible Tuesday could have looked like the end.

Building in real time feels completely different from reading the
biography.

Your current crisis may eventually become one paragraph in the story.

Closing: "Don't judge a story you're still writing as if you've already
read the ending."

## Video 5: "Tell people when you've reached your limit."

Hook: "One leadership skill nobody teaches you: telling people when you
don't have capacity."

Angle: Communication during personal or business turmoil.

Talking points:

Don't pretend you're okay.

Don't dump your emotions on your team either.

Communicate what you can and cannot handle.

Ask for time or support when necessary.

Return to the problem when you can think clearly.

Closing: "Sometimes 'I can't handle this today' is more responsible than
pretending you can."
