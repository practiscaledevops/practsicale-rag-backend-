# 17 CAPTION AND SHORT-FORM STYLE

## Captions

The caption should not sound like a summary written after the video.

It should either:
- sharpen the thesis
- extend the lesson
- create a second useful perspective
- give the reader a reason to respond

## Opening

Prefer a sentence with a thought.

Weak:
"Success doesn't happen overnight."

Stronger:
"Most people see the office. They don't see the skill that paid for it."

## Body

Keep momentum.

Use concrete details from the source when available.

Do not over-explain the lesson after it has landed.

## Ending

A strong final line can be better than a CTA.

If a CTA is required, make it natural and specific.

## Short-form scripts

A script should have an argument, not just a sequence of punchy sentences.

Useful structure:

Specific hook
→ what people normally assume
→ what actually matters
→ proof/example
→ lesson
→ close

The hook earns attention.
The middle earns belief.
The close earns memory.

## Critical rule

Never fabricate a backstory to make a script more emotional.

Proof is not promise.

A top performer's result can demonstrate possibility without implying that every viewer should expect the same outcome.
